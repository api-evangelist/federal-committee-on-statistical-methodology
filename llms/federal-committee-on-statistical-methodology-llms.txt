# Federal Committee on Statistical Methodology (FCSM)

> The Federal Committee on Statistical Methodology is an interagency committee, chartered
> by the Office of Management and Budget and supported by the OMB Statistical Policy Office,
> dedicated to improving the quality of U.S. Federal statistics. FCSM is a policy and
> methodology body: it publishes guidance, standards and conference proceedings. It does not
> operate, publish, or document a public developer API.

## What this is

- This file is written and maintained by [API Evangelist](https://apievangelist.com), not by FCSM.
- It describes a third-party profile of FCSM's **public** surface, assembled from pages any
  member of the public can reach with a browser and no credentials.

## API surface

**None.** As of 2026-09-09, FCSM publishes no API, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/gRPC/WSDL
contract, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no SDK, and no developer portal. Contract discovery
probed the FCSM site host (statspolicy.gov and www), the FCSM vanity domain (fcsm.gov and www,
which 302s to https://statspolicy.gov/FCSM/) and the legacy FCSM host nces.ed.gov for
`/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/v1/openapi.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`,
`/redoc`, `/llms.txt` and the named `/.well-known/` paths. Every `/.well-known/` path returned
404; every other path on statspolicy.gov returned an identical HTML shell (a soft-404 catch-all).

FCSM's published outputs are PDF guidance documents — for example *A Framework for Data
Quality* (FCSM 20-04) and the *AI-Ready Extension to the Data Quality Framework* (FCSM 25-03).
Statistical data itself is published by FCSM's member agencies (Census, BLS, BEA, NCES, NCHS and
others), each of which runs its own API under its own domain; those surfaces belong to those
agencies, not to FCSM.

## Official FCSM pages

- [FCSM home](https://statspolicy.gov/FCSM/): the committee's current site, hosted under the
  OMB Statistical Policy Office site statspolicy.gov (fcsm.gov redirects here).
- [About FCSM](https://statspolicy.gov/FCSM/about/): mission, membership and history.
- [OMB Charter of the FCSM](https://statspolicy.gov/FCSM/about/charter/): the committee's charter.
- [Resources](https://statspolicy.gov/FCSM/resources/): frameworks, reports and guidance.
- [Events and Proceedings](https://statspolicy.gov/FCSM/events/): conferences, seminars, proceedings.
- [Groups Overview](https://statspolicy.gov/FCSM/groups/): subcommittees and working groups.
- [FCSM Research and Policy Conference](https://fcsmconf.org/): the annual conference site.
- [Legacy FCSM site](https://nces.ed.gov/FCSM/): the former FCSM site, hosted by the National
  Center for Education Statistics (U.S. Department of Education); it points to the new home.
